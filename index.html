I'm Truly Sorry, Puja

Puja,

Aaj main dil se tumse sorry bolna chahta hoon.

Mujhse galti ho gayi jab maine woh baat tumhe bata di jo shayad mujhe nahi batani chahiye thi. Mera intention kabhi bhi problem create karna nahi tha, lekin meri wajah se situation awkward ho gayi — aur uske liye main genuinely maafi chahta hoon. Kisi ko bhi tumhare bare mai aaise bolne nhi de sakta tha jab maine tumhe jana toh tum kitni aachi insaan ho.

Kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon, kuch log bas friend category me fit nahi hote. Tumne college ke checkup ke waqt jo kiya, wo sirf help nahi thi… wo presence thi. Aur presence sabke paas nahi hoti. Main labels me believe nahi karta, isliye define bhi nahi kar pa raha… bas itna jaanta hoon ki tumhari jagah mere liye usual nahi hai.

Thank you itna support karne ke liye.

Thank you meri care karne ke liye.

Aur sorry agar maine tumhe hurt kiya ho.

I truly respect you and value our friendship.

– From someone who genuinely cares 🤍

Made with honesty.
